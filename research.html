01 / Research

Topology, Regularity, and \(C^*\)-algebras

My research lies at the interface of topology and the structure theory of \(C^*\)-algebras, with particular emphasis on homotopy phenomena for Cuntz classes and on the role of regularity properties such as pureness and \(\mathcal{Z}\)-stability. I am also interested in topological obstructions and pathological phenomena that arise in the absence of regularity.
